# Blue Current

> Blue Current is a leader in next-generation solid-state battery technology, pioneering fully dry, silicon-first elastic composite batteries that offer high energy density, inherent safety, low operating pressure, and cost-effective scalability for electric vehicles, wearables, and stationary storage.

## Key Battery Highlights

- **Proprietary Elastic Composite:** Proprietary elastic polymers in the composite solve the industry’s expansion problem and increase energy density.
- **Inherent Safety & Efficiency:** Contains no flammable liquids or gels, eliminating fire risks and removing expensive, time-consuming manufacturing steps.
- **Silicon-First Anode:** Silicon is Earth-abundant, high-capacity, and inherently safe.
- **Performance Metrics:** 
  - **More Power:** 750+ Wh/L energy density.
  - **More Cycles:** 2,000+ full depth cycles.
  - **Less Cost:** 20% lower cost compared to conventional alternatives.
